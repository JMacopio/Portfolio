About

Grounded in fundamentals, driven by building things

A quick look at how I got here and what I focus on.

I'm an Information Technology student with a focus on software development, applied AI, and interactive systems. My coursework covers programming, databases, networking, and systems analysis — but most of what I know, I've learned by shipping projects end to end.

My main body of work is Anatomia 3D, an educational Unity application that helps Radiologic Technology students learn human anatomy through interactive 3D models, quizzes, and progress tracking. Building it has meant working across the full stack: Unity and C# on the front end, Firebase Authentication and Firestore for data and accounts, and the Firebase AI Logic SDK for AI-assisted features.

I enjoy the kind of problem where a system has to work under real constraints — a free-tier database, a touch interface, a classroom of actual students — and I like debugging just as much as building.

  • Comfortable across the stack: gameplay/UI logic, cloud data, and backend integration
  • Strong debugging habits — I document and work through issues systematically
  • Aiming toward a software development role where I can keep building applied, real-world tools
